Code Up

I've always been someone who learns by doing, but early on in my CS story I didn't have a lot of resources to practice coding. That's the idea behind CodeUp, provide practice questions for students to reinforce ideas in intro level Computer Science Courses.

PhReddit

I built a Reddit-style platform with a React frontend makes it easy for users to post, join communities, and find what they're looking for, and a backend that runs on Express and MongoDB. The project is locked down with JWT tokens, and wrapped everything in Docker to make testing and deployment smooth across several servers.

Investofolio

Investofolio came about from a conversation I had with a friends, we though there were no good tools for people who wanted to get into investing but didn't have the experience of time to buy a book or take a class. Therefore, Investofolio focused on educating users about stocks, featuring interactive graphs, real world examples and animations.

Lilota

While working with IoT systems, I created an MQTT client integration for Lilota's embedded systems that enables real-time device monitoring through Adafruit's services. I also helped improve an open-source TCL framework by adding networking features that make it easier for devices to communicate and share data.
